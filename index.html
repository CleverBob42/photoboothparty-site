The photo booth nobody has to touch

Stand your iPad or iPhone by the TV. When guests step close, Photo Booth Party counts down, snaps three photos, and puts them in front of party backgrounds with no green screen. The photos play on your TV, and guests save theirs to their phones. Birthdays, Christmas, Halloween, New Year, weddings, office parties: any party.

Everyone gets in the photo, nobody fiddles with the device

Set it up once, then enjoy your own party.

Completely hands-free

Stand it by the TV and guests never touch it. It sees when people step close and starts a 20-second countdown by itself, in any orientation.

Three photos, three poses

Each visit is a burst of three photos, with a clear “Photo 1 of 3” and a 3-2-1 between shots.

Backgrounds, no green screen

An on-device person cut-out puts guests in front of confetti, balloons, snowy cabins or haunted mansions. Some rounds use the real room.

Party on the big screen

With AirPlay to an Apple TV, the newest burst plays as a flip-book, then a slideshow of the whole party with a QR code.

Scan, save, share

Each burst becomes a photo strip and an animated GIF, saved to Photos. Guests on your Wi-Fi scan the QR code to open a gallery and save their own photos.

Private by design

No accounts and nothing is uploaded to the internet. The gallery is served by your device itself to your Wi-Fi.

A theme for every party

Each theme brings its own backgrounds and its own wording. Type an event name like “Happy 40th Sam!” and it's printed on every photo strip.

How it works

Set up in a few minutes, then the booth runs itself.

  1. Prop it up

    Stand your iPad or iPhone by the TV and start AirPlay mirroring to your Apple TV.

  2. Step in and pose

    Guests step in front of the device and pose. The countdown and the photos start by themselves.

  3. Watch the TV

    The newest photos flip through on the TV, then join a slideshow of the whole party with a QR code.

  4. Scan to save

    Guests on your Wi-Fi scan the QR code to open the gallery and save their own strip or GIF.

You stay in charge

Tap the photo count for the host admin: delete bursts, choose the theme and event name, switch backgrounds on or off, delete them, add your own from Photos, and use the Stop/Start button while you set up. Guests can also send their own background picture from their phone, and it's used next. Turn that off any time.
