Everyone gets in the photo, nobody fiddles with the iPad

Set it up once, then enjoy your own party.

Completely hands-free

It sees when people step close and starts a 20-second countdown by itself. Guests never need to touch the iPad.

Three photos, three poses

Each visit is a burst of three photos, with a clear “Photo 1 of 3” and a 3-2-1 between shots.

Spooky scenes, no green screen

An on-device person cut-out puts guests in front of a haunted graveyard, gothic mansion, foggy forest, pumpkin house and more. Some rounds use the real room.

Party on the big screen

With AirPlay to an Apple TV, the newest burst plays as a flip-book, then a slideshow of the whole party.

Scan, save, share

Each burst becomes a photo strip and an animated GIF, saved to the iPad's Photos. Guests on your Wi-Fi scan a QR code on the TV to save theirs to their phone.

Private by design

No accounts and nothing is uploaded to the internet. The gallery is served by the iPad itself to your home Wi-Fi.

How it works

Set up in a few minutes, then the booth runs itself.

  1. Prop it up

    Prop the iPad by the TV and start AirPlay mirroring to your Apple TV.

  2. Step in and pose

    Guests step in front of the iPad and pose. The countdown and the photos start by themselves.

  3. Watch the TV

    The photos appear on the TV, then join a slideshow of the whole party with a QR code.

  4. Scan to save

    Guests on your Wi-Fi scan the QR code to save their own strip or GIF to their phone.

You stay in charge

Tap the photo count for the host admin: delete bursts, switch backgrounds on or off, delete them, add your own from Photos, and use the Stop/Start button while you set up. Guests can also send their own background picture from their phone, and it's used next. Turn that off any time.
