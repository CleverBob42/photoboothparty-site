Frequently asked questions

The TV isn't showing the photos.

Start Screen Mirroring from Control Center on the iPad and pick your Apple TV, then reopen the Spooky Booth app.

Guests can't open the QR code.

Their phones must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPad. The gallery is served by the iPad itself, so it only works on your home network while the app is open.

How do I delete photos?

Tap the photo count in the app to open the host admin, where you can delete bursts.

The camera is off or the booth can't see anyone.

Open iPadOS Settings → Spooky Booth and make sure Camera is switched on.
